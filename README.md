## 🚑 Smart Urban Traffic & Emergency Response System

### Overview

An AI-powered software platform designed to improve emergency vehicle response by analyzing traffic conditions, road incidents, and available routes.

### Problem

Emergency vehicles such as ambulances can be delayed due to:

- Heavy traffic congestion
- Road accidents and blockages
- Changing traffic conditions
- Lack of emergency-aware route optimization
- Delayed traffic management decisions

### Our Solution

Our system analyzes traffic and incident data to identify a suitable emergency route.

The system workflow is:

Emergency Request
        ↓
Traffic & Incident Analysis
        ↓
Congestion Prediction
        ↓
Route Optimization
        ↓
Route Comparison
        ↓
Emergency Corridor
        ↓
Dynamic Re-routing
        ↓
Destination

### Key Features

- 🚑 Emergency vehicle management
- 🗺️ Traffic-aware route optimization
- 🧠 AI-based congestion prediction
- 🚨 Accident and incident detection
- 🔄 Dynamic route recalculation
- 🚦 Emergency corridor generation
- 📊 Interactive traffic dashboard
- ⏱️ ETA estimation
- 🚦 Traffic signal priority recommendations
